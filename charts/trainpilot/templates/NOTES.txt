Create a task:

  kubectl apply -f config/samples/trainpilot_v1alpha1_traintask.yaml

Then watch it:

  kubectl -n <namespace> get traintasks -w

Notes:
  - Queueing, gang admission and billing are operator-side: nothing else needs
    Kueue or Volcano installed.
  - Stop / continue / rerun a task with
    kubectl annotate traintask <name> trainpilot.wutz.dev/action=stop=$(date +%s)
    kubectl annotate traintask <name> trainpilot.wutz.dev/action=continue=$(date +%s)
    kubectl annotate traintask <name> trainpilot.wutz.dev/action=rerun=$(date +%s)
